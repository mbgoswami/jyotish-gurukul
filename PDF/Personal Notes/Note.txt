**Museum Information:**
The Crafts Museum celebrates the rich heritage of Kutch crafts, currently focusing on over forty distinct styles of hand embroidery practiced by 12 different communities. These are showcased in our current exhibition, *The Living Embroideries of Kutch*, featuring large-size embroidery panels created by contemporary craftswomen. The technical mastery and creativity in these works are unparalleled in the modern world.

The museum also features a Hands-on Gallery and Crafts Studios, and is part of Shrujan’s Living and Learning Design Centre (LLDC) – a unique resource dedicated to preserving and promoting Kutch crafts, ensuring sustainable livelihoods for craftspeople. We also invite you to visit our Crafts Shop and Café during your trip.
